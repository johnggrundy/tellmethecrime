Episode 32

The Hitman Who Became the Hit - The Susan Kuhnhausen Case

A man is waiting inside a woman’s bedroom with a hammer.

He’s been sent there to kill her.

But about fourteen minutes later, only one of them is still alive.

And it isn’t the hitman.

In this episode, John tells Febriana the case of Susan Kuhnhausen, a woman who fought back against the man hired to murder her and survived a plot that led directly back to her estranged husband.

Listener discretion is advised. This episode discusses attempted murder, domestic violence, and graphic violence.
