Episode 30

The Husband Who Killed His Wife’s Killer - The Mark Winger Case

When Mark Winger finds his wife being brutally attacked inside their home, he shoots and kills her attacker. Police quickly view him as a grieving husband who tried to save his wife, and the case appears straightforward.

But one small piece of evidence suggests the supposed killer may have been invited there. Years later, a secret affair and a witness with a disturbing story force investigators to reconsider everything they thought they knew about the crime scene.

In this episode, John tells Febriana the case of Donnah Winger and Roger Harrington.
