Episode 33

The Garage at the End of the Alley - The Murder of Johnny Altinger

In 2008, an aspiring filmmaker in Edmonton was working on a horror movie about a man who is lured to a garage and killed. Not long after, a real man disappeared after being directed to that very same garage.

When police began investigating, they discovered that the similarities didn’t end there. There was another man, another encounter - and a disturbing story hidden on the filmmaker’s computer.

So how much of this was fiction, and how much had actually happened?

In this episode, Febriana tells John the case of Johnny Altinger.
