Episode 31

The Pizza Delivery Man with a Bomb Around His Neck - The Brian Wells Case

Imagine a pizza delivery driver walks into a bank with a bomb locked around his neck.

He says he has been forced into a bizarre scavenger hunt: rob the bank, follow a series of handwritten instructions, and complete each step before the bomb’s timer runs out.

But as investigators try to understand who had built the device and why, the story becomes much more complicated. Evidence begins pointing toward a group of people connected by money, murder, and an elaborate plan that may not have unfolded the way anyone intended.

Today on Tell Me The Crime Episode 31, Febriana tells John the story of Brian Wells. 

Listener discretion is advised. This episode discusses murder, explosives, and  death.
